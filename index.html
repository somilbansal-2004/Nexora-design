About Nexora

Engineering discipline, design sensibility

Nexora Engineering and Design is a multidisciplinary studio delivering mechanical design, CAD modeling, product development, electrical design, automation and creative design under one roof. We take a concept from a rough sketch to a manufacturing-ready file, and a brand from an idea to a finished visual identity.

Founded by Somil Bansal — engineer, designer, innovator — Nexora exists at the intersection of precise engineering and considered design, built for clients who need both to work together.
